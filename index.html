The Rise of Urban Vertical Farming

[Paragraph A] By the year 2050, nearly 80% of the Earth's population is projected to reside in urban centres. Applying conservative estimates to current demographic trends, the human population will increase by about 3 billion people during the interim. An estimated 109 hectares of new land (about 20% larger than Brazil) will be needed to grow enough food to feed them, if traditional farming practices continue as they are practiced today.

[Paragraph B] Vertical farming—growing crops in indoor, stacked layers under controlled artificial lighting and hydroponic nutrient systems—has emerged as a viable solution. Proposed in 1999 by Dickson Despommier, a professor of Public and Environmental Health at Columbia University, the concept aims to produce food year-round without reliance on seasonal weather patterns or chemical pesticides.

[Paragraph C] Compared to conventional outdoor agriculture, indoor vertical farms consume up to 95% less water due to closed-loop recycling systems. However, critics point out that the high electricity demand required for full-spectrum LED lighting makes staple crops like wheat and rice economically unfeasible at present, limiting commercial vertical farms primarily to fast-growing leafy greens and herbs.

Hinchingbrooke Country Park — Luyện Nghe Điền Từ

📜 Audio Script (Cambridge 19 Test 1 Part 1):

"Hello everyone. Hinchingbrooke Country Park covers an area of 69 hectares and consists of two main habitats: a large wetland area to the west and an ancient woodland to the east. For school educational visits, children studying Science look at different types of habitat and collect data on water quality. In the Geography session, students learn how to use a map and compass. Educational visits give children a feeling of freedom that they may not experience in a classroom."

Write ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER for each answer.

📐 Dàn Bài Chuẩn YouPass Writing (4 Đoạn Rõ Ràng):

1. Introduction (2 câu): Paraphrase câu hỏi + Thesis Statement trả lời trực diện.

2. Body 1 (5 câu): Luận điểm 1 + Giải thích bản chất + Ví dụ thực tế.

3. Body 2 (5 câu): Luận điểm 2 + Tác động dài hạn đối với xã hội.

4. Conclusion (1-2 câu): Khẳng định lại quan điểm bằng cấu trúc In conclusion, while..., I firmly believe...

1. Công thức A.R.E.A (Part 1 & Part 3)

Answer (Trả lời thẳng) ➔ Reason (Lý do: The primary reason is that...) ➔ Example (Ví dụ cá nhân: For instance, just last weekend...).

2. Trục Thời Gian P.P.F (Part 2 Nói Đủ 2 Phút)

Chia bài nói thành Past (Kỷ niệm quá khứ - 30s) ➔ Present (Miêu tả hiện tại - 60s) ➔ Future (Dự định tương lai - 30s) để ăn điểm đa dạng thì.

3. Câu "Câu Giờ" Tự Nhiên Khi Gặp Câu Khó

"That's a really intriguing question; I've never looked at it from that angle before, but I suppose..."

4. Tự Sửa Khi Lỡ Miệng (Self-Correction = Điểm Cộng)

Nói ngay: "Or rather, what I meant to say was..." — Giám khảo IDP cộng điểm Fluency khi bạn biết tự sửa tự nhiên.
